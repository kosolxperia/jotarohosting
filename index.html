Refund & Cancellation Policy

Transparent guidelines regarding deposits, milestone approvals, and cancellations.

Our Commitment to Chargeback Prevention & Fair Billing

Because software development involves customized human engineering and cloud resources, our refund policies are structured to reflect completed work stages. We work transparently through milestone sign-offs to ensure complete client satisfaction before proceeding to subsequent project phases.

1. Upfront Retainer / Deposit Refundability

  • Prior to Work Initiation: If a client requests cancellation within 72 hours of paying the initial deposit and prior to the commencement of discovery or development work, a 100% full refund (minus third-party payment processing fees) will be issued.
  • After Work Has Commenced: Once research, wireframing, architecture planning, or software engineering work has begun, the initial deposit becomes non-refundable to cover expended resource hours.

2. Project Milestones & Interim Work

Custom development projects are billed in stages tied to verifiable milestones (e.g., Design Approval, Beta Release, Production Launch). Once a milestone sign-off is submitted by the client and the associated invoice is paid, that payment is considered final and non-refundable.

3. Client-Initiated Project Cancellation

Clients may cancel an active development project at any time by providing formal written notice to support@jotarostrategy.com. Upon receipt of cancellation notice:

  • The client will be invoiced for all unbilled hours or partial milestones completed up to the cancellation date.
  • All completed design work, wireframes, code repositories, and documentation created up to the point of cancellation will be transferred to the client upon settlement of outstanding fees.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Eligible refunds approved under this policy will be processed back to the original card or payment account used during checkout via Stripe. Processing times typically take 5 to 10 business days depending on your financial institution.

5. Dispute Resolution

If you have any questions or dissatisfaction regarding billed work, please reach out to our management team at support@jotarostrategy.com before initiating a payment chargeback. We are dedicated to working directly with clients to resolve discrepancies fairly.
